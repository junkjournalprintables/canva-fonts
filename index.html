Common questions

Can I use these fonts in Canva?

Yes — download the font file from Creative Fabrica, then upload it to Canva under Brand Kit → Fonts (Canva Pro) or add it to your computer for the desktop app. These are standalone fonts, not Canva's built-in library, so check each licence for commercial use.

Why Creative Fabrica instead of free font sites?

Every font here includes a commercial licence and comes in one place, so you can pair a script with a serif without hunting across sites. The All Access trial lets you test a whole set before committing.

How do I pair two fonts?

Pick one script or display font for the headline and one clean serif or simple handwriting for the body. Most combinations on this page come from the Script and Serif sections — one from each is a reliable Canva pairing.

Do the prices and counts stay the same?

Save counts and availability were accurate when this page was built and change often — open the product page for current details and pricing.
